# TryHackMe Rooms Write-ups

![TryHackMe Banner](https://tryhackme.com/img/share-card.png)

## Overview

This repository contains my personal collection of notes, scripts, and detailed write-ups for the rooms I have completed on the [TryHackMe](https://tryhackme.com/) platform. The main goal is to document my learning process, reinforce concepts, and serve as a reference for future challenges.

---

## Purpose

The primary purposes of this repository are:

* **Learning & Reinforcement:** Documenting the steps taken helps solidify my understanding of cybersecurity concepts and tools.
* **Tracking Progress:** To maintain a log of all the rooms I have successfully completed.
* **Portfolio:** To showcase my skills, methodology, and dedication to continuous learning in the cybersecurity field.

---

## Repository Structure

The repository is organized by room name. Each room has its own directory, containing relevant files such as:

* **`README.md` or `notes.md`:** A detailed write-up of the steps taken to complete the room, including enumeration, exploitation, and privilege escalation.
* **`files/`:** Any other relevant files, such as wordlists, exploit code, or important screenshots.

Example structure:
.
├── TRYHACKME-ROOMS/
│   ├── README.md
│   └── Pyramid-Of-Pain/
│       └── Introduction.md
│       └── screenshot.png
│   └── Cyber-Security/
│       └── Intro.md
│       └── Cyber-Attacks.md
└── ...

## Disclaimer

The content in this repository is intended for educational and ethical purposes only. 

The solutions and write-ups are my own and reflect the methods I used at the time of completion. They should be used as a reference or learning aid, not as a direct shortcut to completing the rooms.
